WAVE DASH - PLAY FREE NEON RHYTHM PLATFORMER

Welcome to Wave Dash, a free browser-based arcade rhythm platformer. Fly your glowing neon spaceship through challenging geometry-themed tunnels, dodge moving sawblades, avoid electric spikes, and glide to the beat of the high-energy soundtrack. Master your reflex timing, unlock stunning shop rewards, and test your skills across multiple levels. No download is needed—start playing in your browser right now!

HOW TO PLAY

  • Jump/Fly Upwards: Press Spacebar, click your Left Mouse Button, or tap the screen.
  • Descend: Release the key/tap to let gravity guide your ship down.
  • Dodge Obstacles: Avoid all red neon spikes, sawblades, and electric barriers. One crash will reset your attempt!
  • Collect Coins: Grab golden coins scattered along the path to spend on legendary skins in the Shop.

GAME FEATURES

  • Rhythm-Based Action: Obstacles and patterns synchronized beautifully with neon rhythm tracks.
  • Practice Mode: Place checkpoints using Z and remove them with X to master hard sections.
  • Custom Level Editor: Build your own levels from scratch, customize obstacle patterns, and test them instantly!
  • Coin Investment System: Put your hard-earned coins to work! Invest them in the menu to earn passive interest hourly.
  • Premium Upgrades: Unlock golden skins, ad-free passes, and custom profile icons.

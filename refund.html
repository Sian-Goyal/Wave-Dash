Last updated: July 29, 2026

Thank you for playing and purchasing upgrades in Wave Dash.

1. Digital Goods Delivery

All items sold inside Wave Dash (such as Coins, Game History key, or Golden Pass upgrade) are intangible digital assets. They are delivered instantly and automatically to your local game profile upon successful payment validation by PayPal or Razorpay.

2. Refund Eligibility

Due to the instant delivery nature of digital goods, all purchases are generally non-refundable once the transaction is marked successful by the gateway. This includes accidental purchases, buyer's remorse, or unauthorized transactions made by minors or children using a parent's credit card or payment device. Parents and guardians are responsible for the secure storage and authorization of their payment credentials.

3. Technical Errors & Support

If you experience any technical difficulties where your payment was successful but the items did not credit to your profile, please contact us at khadariasian@gmail.com within 7 days of the purchase. Provide your transaction reference ID, and we will verify the transaction logs to manually credit the items or issue a full refund where applicable.

📜 PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 29, 2026

At Wave Dash, accessible from https://wavedashgame-1.vercel.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Wave Dash and how we use it.

1. Information Collection and Use

To enable score synchronization and profile recovery, we collect your username and password (which is stored securely in our Google Firebase database). We do not require any other personal identification like real names, phone numbers, or email addresses unless you contact us directly.

2. Payment Processing (PayPal & Razorpay)

All virtual coin purchases and premium membership updates are handled securely via our payment gateway partners, PayPal and Razorpay. We do NOT collect, save, or have access to any of your financial information (credit card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI details). They process all transactions independently and securely.

3. Cookies and Log Files

Wave Dash uses standard local storage and cache to remember your game saves, skins, high scores, and settings. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising profiling.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions about our privacy policies, please contact us at khadariasian@gmail.com.
